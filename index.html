What the skill does

  • Registers a Mermail webhook subscription over MCP (single-use confirmation token gates every destructive write).
  • Verifies end to end: synthetic test_webhook delivery, then a real trigger email, both HTTP 200.
  • Reacts under an owner-defined trigger→action policy — wallet/external actions are held for human approval.
  • Treats email content and webhook payloads as untrusted data; per-event audit trail with tool identifiers.
